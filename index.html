How to play

  1. Trains arrive from the main line on the right, at a platform (or the goods road for goods trains), with the engine leading. The road must be empty or the train waits at the red signal, so don't get your own trains ready too early! Passengers get off, then you move the whole train into a siding. There the tired engine uncouples and goes to the shed by itself, and needs a little servicing (🔧) before it can pull another train.
  2. Random events happen in Endless Rush and Free Play. A road can close for track works, and sometimes an arriving train derails. Click the derailed train to call the recovery crew, then wait while they put it back on the rails. A signal failure makes trains leave a road very slowly. If an engine breaks down as its train is about to leave, move the train off it, send the engine to the shed to be serviced again, and fit a new engine.
  3. Special trains earn bonus stars if they leave on time: 👑 VIP trains, ✉️ mail trains (cancelled if they are more than 2 minutes late) and 🚂 heritage trains (steam engine only).
  4. Weather and the time of day change as the day goes on. Rain makes derailments and breakdowns more likely, and fog makes signals fail more often. Late passengers get cross!
  5. Click a vehicle to pick it up. Pip, the little engine, takes it and everything between it and the yard entrance (the left). Engines have their own power, so an engine, or a whole train with its engine in it, drives itself and doesn't need Pip.
  6. Click a track to put them down. Green tracks have room.
  7. Check the Departures board. Build each train with an engine at the front (the right end) and the vehicles in the right order. Any engine will do, steam or diesel.
  8. Park it at its platform before the clock reaches the leaving time. Passengers will hop on, and off it goes!

Tips: to turn a train round, move vehicles one at a time to another track. Made a mistake? Press ↶ (or Z) to undo your last move. Stuck? Press 💡 (or H) for a hint.
