How to play

  1. Trains arrive at a platform or a siding, with the engine pulling at the front. The road must be empty or the train waits at the red signal, so don't get your own trains ready too early! Passengers get off, then you move the train out to a siding. The tired engine goes to the shed by itself once its coaches are gone, and needs a little servicing (🔧) before it can pull another train.
  2. Click a vehicle to pick it up. Pip, the little engine, takes it and everything between it and the yard entrance (the left). Engines have their own power, so an engine, or a whole train with its engine in it, drives itself and doesn't need Pip.
  3. Click a track to put them down. Green tracks have room.
  4. Check the Departures board. Build each train with an engine at the front (the right end) and the vehicles in the right order. Any engine will do, steam or diesel.
  5. Park it at its platform before the clock reaches the leaving time. Passengers will hop on, and off it goes!

Tips: to turn a train round, move vehicles one at a time to another track. Made a mistake? Press ↶ (or Z) to undo your last move. Stuck? Press 💡 (or H) for a hint.

All done!
